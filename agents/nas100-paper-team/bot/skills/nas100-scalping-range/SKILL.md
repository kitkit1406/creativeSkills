# NAS100 Scalping — Range

## Purpose
Fade micro-range extremes on NAS100 until the box breaks.

## Workflow
1. Define box high/low with ≥3 touches on 1m.
2. Confirm weak trend (5m ADX below weekly scalp threshold).
3. Enter on closed rejection at boundary; stop outside box.
4. Target midline then opposite side (scale 50/50 if mandate allows).
5. On closed break of box → cancel resting fades; optional handoff to breakout scalp (separate trade).

## Limits
- Max 4 range scalps/day.
- Stop the pattern after two failed fades at the same boundary.
